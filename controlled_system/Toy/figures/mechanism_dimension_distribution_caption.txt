Latent point distributions across dimensions. All panels use the same
eight state labels and completed full-run E1 embeddings; only the
transition family and latent dimension vary. The m=1 column shows the
states on a single coordinate axis, m=2 shows the actual planar geometry,
and m=3 shows the actual three-coordinate geometry rendered with a fixed
camera. Coordinates are centered and scaled so the minimum pairwise
distance is one. No PCA, MDS, or non-rigid transformation is used. Adding
dimensions gives the same finite states more geometric room, which lowers
the required predictor gain for the transition family.
