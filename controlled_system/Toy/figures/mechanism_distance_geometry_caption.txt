How dimension changes the latent geometry available for prediction.
All panels contain the same eight state labels; only the transition family
and latent dimension vary. Each heatmap shows the true pairwise Euclidean
distances of the best-found latent geometry. Higher dimension allows the
geometry to become more compatible with the transition constraints: the
cyclic system reaches gain one in two dimensions, while the transposition
families require increasingly uniform pairwise distances and reach gain
one at the simplex dimension m=7. The additional dimensions do not encode
new state information; they provide geometric degrees of freedom for
realizing the transition family.
