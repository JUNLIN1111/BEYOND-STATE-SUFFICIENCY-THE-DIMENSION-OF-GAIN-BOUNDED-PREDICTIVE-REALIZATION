Transition arrows and bounded-gain prediction error. All panels use the
same eight state labels and completed full-run E1 embeddings. Arrows show
one true transition action in the latent geometry: the cyclic action for
Cycle and the hard-gain witness action for the two transposition families.
Solid black segments mark the current witness pair, and dashed black
segments mark its successor pair under that same action. The reported
prediction error is the finite-state numerical oracle MSE at gain budget
L=1, averaged over all actions in the transition family. As dimension gives
the states more geometric room, the required gain and the constrained
prediction error both decrease.
